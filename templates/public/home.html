A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Choose a date and an available time, enter your details, and submit your request. The clinic will confirm your appointment.

What should I bring?

Bring any relevant previous reports, your current medication list, and questions you would like to discuss.

Can I cancel my appointment?

Yes. Use the private link on your booking receipt or in your email before the appointment starts. For other changes, call the clinic.

Do I need an account?

No. You can book directly without creating an account.
